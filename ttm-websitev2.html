How a position gets chosen

Every trade follows the same sequence, whether it's held for two hours or two weeks.

Discover

Screen for opportunities using technical setups, fundamentals, economic conditions, news flow, and market data.

Analyze

Evaluate the setup, define an entry point, a target, and the potential downside before any capital moves.

Size the risk

Set position size and a stop-loss based on the account and the individual investor's comfort with loss.

Execute

Enter the position through the brokerage and monitor it actively while it's open.

Review

Exit at the target, when the thesis changes, or when the risk limit requires the position to close.

Trading approach

Swing trading

The primary approach — positions are typically held for a few days to a few weeks.

Day trading

Shorter-term positions are taken when a setup calls for it, sometimes closed within the same session.

How fees work

Two simple components. No fee is charged on a loss.

1%

Management fee

Charged against assets under management, regardless of performance in a given period.

10%

Performance fee

TTM takes 10% of realized profit. Example: a $1,000 gain results in a $100 fee, leaving $900 for the investor.

0%

Fee on losses

If the portfolio doesn't produce a profit over a period, no performance fee is charged. Losses are borne by the investor.

Risk comes first. Returns come second.

2%

Planned maximum loss on a single trade, or less if that's what the investor is comfortable with.

Sized

Position sizing is set per trade and per portfolio — not left uncapped.

Stop-loss

Trades carry a predetermined exit level to help contain downside.

Aligned

Risk exposure is matched to what each investor says they can tolerate losing.

Questions people ask

Is TTM accepting money right now?

No. TTM is currently a personal, simulated project. Nothing on this site creates an investment account or moves any money. The application below joins a waitlist only.

How would deposits actually work if TTM launches?

The concept is that an investor's funds would sit in an account trades are executed through, with TTM handling the trading decisions. That structure isn't active today.

What's the minimum to get started?

As currently designed, the concept proposes a $100 minimum — subject to change before any real launch.

Can I see my trades?

The plan is for investors to be able to ask about their portfolio and trades directly at any time, rather than a always-on live dashboard.

Can I withdraw whenever I want?

That's the intent behind the design — withdrawals whenever an investor chooses, within whatever the actual account structure allows once this is real.
